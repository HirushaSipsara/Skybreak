    YOUR IS EXPLANATION

    Sense → decide → act → repeat.

    Both are autonomous, rule-based agents. Their decisions respond to game state. They do not use machine learning or train a neural network.

    01 / RIFT WARDEN

    Utility selection inside a state machine

    While pursuing, the boss compares eligible cannon, blade and teleport scores. It enters one action state, which runs its animation markers and damage windows. It then recovers and chooses again.

    PERCEPTION → ELIGIBILITY → SCORES
    ↓
    ACTION → RECOVERY → NEXT DECISION

    The phase is derived from arm health. A broken arm interrupts its current attack. The core stays protected until both arms are gone and the opening reaches 1.5 seconds.

    02 / RELAY

    A utility-based support agent

    Relay observes the player's health, its own health, distance, visible threats and cooldowns. It calculates follow, heal, defend and retreat scores. It can change action at each sensing tick.

    follow = base + clamp(distance / 9) × weight
    heal = base + missingHealth × weight
    defend = base + danger × weight
    retreat = configured utility, when eligible

    Healing can win while the player is out of range: Relay approaches first. Retreat requires low own health and recent threat memory. Higher scores alone do not bypass those conditions.

    03 / THE REAR ATTACK

    It reads your facing twice.

    1. After 0.18 seconds, sample the exact position behind your current facing.
    2. Check NavMesh, walls, arena boundary and nearby actors. Cancel if blocked.
    3. Wait the configured rear delay (default one second).
    4. Read your facing again and teleport directly behind you again.
    5. Apply one immediate close strike, then recover.

    Turning changes the second landing. The landing distance keeps the boss capsules clear. There is no search around a ring for this attack.

    04 / CORE PHASE

    Warn. Lock. Slam.

    The boss samples up to eight valid core landing candidates around the player. Unlike the rear attack, this teleport can choose a clear candidate. A blocked arrival cancels the warp; the action still proceeds into its warned slam, matching the current C# flow.

    The slam warning follows the player's ground location until 1.267 seconds. At 1.5 seconds it applies damage within the configured radius. In Unity, feet more than 1.05 metres above the strike evade it.

    Destroy the exposed core to begin the death state. The return point powers at 2.633 seconds and the boss is defeated at 3 seconds.

    05 / NAVIGATION & COST

    Spend work where it matters.

    NavMeshMotor accepts complete Unity NavMesh paths, samples valid destinations and throttles replans. A stalled agent requests another route. This is Unity pathfinding, not a custom A* implementation.

    Boss sight is cached between perception checks. Attack animation and damage timing still update each frame. Relay throttles scanning and scoring together. The boss already reuses physics query buffers to reduce allocations.

    A longer interval reduces work but adds reaction delay. Validate routes, doors and timing in Unity after tuning.
