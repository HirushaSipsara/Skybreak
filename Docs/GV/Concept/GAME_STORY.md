# Skybreak — The Descent: shared game story

Story direction, 4 October 2026. This document gives the team a common setting, player objective and map progression while leaving agent designs open.

## Story to share with the team

Skybreak's district hospital has lost its main power supply. The only way to restore emergency power is to restart the reactor inside the Aurora Vault, an old underground facility built from stone chambers and narrow passages, with energy machinery added over time.

An unexplained failure has sealed the facility's gates and cut its communication with the surface. Nobody knows exactly what has happened inside. Arc, a rescue engineer, enters the Vault with an energy blaster and a mission: reach the central controls and bring the hospital's emergency systems back online.

The journey begins in the loading court. The entrance checkpoint is blocked, and danger may be waiting near the gate or hidden behind the abandoned equipment. Arc must overcome the first encounter and find a way into the maintenance passage.

The passage winds deeper underground. The lights become dimmer, the machinery falls quiet and the route turns through narrow corners. As Arc reaches the inner security wing, doors open along the sides of the passage. Threats may emerge from those rooms or appear farther along the route. Arc must survive the second encounter and gain access to the reactor chamber.

Beyond the last gate lies the central reactor. Its emergency systems are still locked, and the greatest danger waits in this final area. Arc must overcome the final obstacle and reach the control console.

When Arc restarts the reactor, light spreads through the corridors behind him. Communication with the surface returns. The hospital confirms that emergency power is restored and its critical systems are working again.

The mission ends with a simple message: **“Power restored. People first.”**

## Player journey

**Entrance → first encounter → winding passage → second encounter and side rooms → final chamber → restore power.**

Everything happens in one connected map. The player walks from one section into the next as the mission progresses. The story gives a reason to keep moving deeper into the facility.

## Where enemies might appear

| Location | Possible appearance points |
| --- | --- |
| Entrance/loading court | Near the first checkpoint, around the gate or behind crates and other cover. |
| Winding maintenance passage | Beyond blind corners, inside dark alcoves or at corridor junctions. |
| Side rooms/security wing | Inside the rooms on either side of the route. Doors opening can reveal them or provide a route into the main passage. |
| Second encounter area | Across the main open space, near cover or entering through connected room doors. |
| Final reactor chamber | Near the reactor, around the chamber's entrances or in concealed positions within the final arena. |

These are possible placements, not mandatory spawn points. Each member can decide how their agent fits the environment, then agree placement and progression triggers with the world builder.

## What remains open for member designs

This shared story does not name or assign agents, set a fixed enemy count, require anyone to create a regular hostile enemy, or prescribe appearance, abilities, attack patterns or algorithms. Members choose those details for their own agents and coordinate them with the player journey and their individual assignment requirements.

The cause of the failure and the identity of the threats are deliberately left open so those designs can fit the story. An agent can be introduced through the facility's purpose, its failure or the rescue mission without rewriting the player's main objective.

## Shared presentation

Use a stylised cartoon world that is practical to build in Unity and run on the weakest team laptop. The descent should feel tense through lighting, sound, corners and unexpected appearances. Keep the route and important cues readable so the player understands where to go.

The final objective is always to restore the hospital's emergency power. The existing city/escort demos remain earlier references; the current mission follows this continuous underground route.

This is a planned story and encounter layout. It is not a claim that combat or mission progression is already implemented.
