# Skybreak: The Descent

## Shared science-fantasy story

Beneath the surface lies the Aurora Vault, an ancient fortress built around a crystal engine called the Aether Core. Stone halls, luminous crystals and forgotten machines once kept a passage between worlds stable. Now the Core is fractured. A growing rift is twisting the Vault and threatening to consume the world above.

Arc, a half-human, half-machine pathfinder, descends into the ruins with Relay, a hovering support companion. Arc carries an energy carbine powered by a regenerating reserve. Its cell holds twenty beam shots before it needs a short recharge.

The Vault's guardians still defend their assigned crystals. Corrupted security systems mistake the explorers for intruders. Arc and Relay must cross the outer court, survive the winding maintenance passages and reach the inner sanctum.

The Rift Warden stands between them and the Aether Core. Its cannon and blade protect the unstable heart of the Vault. Arc must destroy both arms, expose the chest core and defeat the guardian.

With the Warden defeated, the Aether Core awakens. Crystal energy spreads through the halls, seals the breach and restores the return gate. Arc and Relay pass through the gate as the Vault stabilizes.

The mission ends with: **Rift sealed. Sky reclaimed.**

## Player journey

Enter the Aurora Vault, cross the outer court, explore the winding passage, survive the security wing, defeat Rift Warden, awaken the Aether Core and return through the gate.

The journey takes place in the existing connected dungeon map. The ending begins automatically when the final boss's core is destroyed.

## Presentation

Use stylized science-fantasy visuals: ancient stone, cyan crystal energy, copper conduits, mechanical guardians and the boss chamber's violet rift energy. Keep the route and combat readable on the weakest team laptop.

Arc's carbine fires energy beams. Its reserve is unlimited; its twenty-shot cell and recharge delay provide the combat limit.

The shared story supplies the setting and mission goal. Each member's agent design and assessment responsibilities remain separate.
